Observations from the Foster Family

In our short time with Tito, we've seen him change from a skiddish street dog to a loving companion. He only wants to love and be a part of a family. Kids are great, other dogs are just as great. He's done well in a kennel with his potty training, and will now indicate that he's wanting to go outside. He's an amazing walking partner with almost no leash pulling; the occasional squirrel or cat is enough to excite him, it's easy to regain his attention and continue the walk. He's great at starting a rough housing session with the other dogs. This is a one in a million dog, ready to make his forever family complete.
